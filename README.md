# Broraa Studio

**Create polished multilingual videos and voice projects in one desktop workspace.**

Broraa Studio brings transcription, translation, voice production, dubbing, and video finishing into a connected workflow. Move from source media to a finished export while keeping your text, timing, audio, and project files organized.

## What you can do

- **Transcribe and refine:** Turn speech into editable segments, review the text against your media, and export subtitles.
- **Translate for your audience:** Prepare translated dialogue and subtitles while keeping each line connected to its original timing.
- **Produce voices and dubbing:** Build a voice library, generate speech for individual segments or a full script, and refine clips on a multitrack timeline.
- **Finish your video:** Preview the result, adjust audio and subtitles, and export a finished video or subtitle files.
- **Handle larger projects:** Process multiple videos with a batch queue, then return to saved projects and production history when you need them.

## Download

Get the latest Windows installer from the [Releases page](https://github.com/kakdev10k/Broraa-Studio-Releases/releases). Open the newest release and download `Broraa-Studio-Setup-<version>.exe`.

After installation, open Broraa Studio and follow the in-app setup prompts. Some creative features require a one-time download from **Settings → AI Models**.
